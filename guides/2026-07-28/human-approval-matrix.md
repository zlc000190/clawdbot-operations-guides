# Clawdbot human approval matrix

[Clawdbot](https://clawdbot.tech/) is the project entry point for this independent self-hosted personal AI agent review template. Confirm current availability, features, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Approval levels

| Level | Example scope | Required review |
|---|---|---|
| Observe | Read public or synthetic inputs | Automated checks plus sampled human review |
| Draft | Produce a reversible proposal | One named reviewer before external use |
| Change | Modify content, configuration, or workflow state | Reviewer checks scope, evidence, and rollback path |
| Consequential | Legal, financial, privacy, publication, or irreversible action | Qualified owner gives explicit final approval |

## Review questions

1. Are the inputs authorized and limited to the stated purpose?
2. Does the evidence include synthetic data, least-privilege tools, action logs, backup evidence, and tested restore steps?
3. Can the reviewer see the exact proposed output or diff?
4. Are cost, uncertainty, side effects, and affected people clear?
5. Is there a tested stop or rollback path?

Silence, a preselected checkbox, or an expired approval is not consent. Record who approved, what version they reviewed, the decision time, and any conditions.
