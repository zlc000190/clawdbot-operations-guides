# Clawdbot change monitoring log

This independent note uses [Clawdbot](https://clawdbot.tech/) as the project entry point for a self-hosted personal AI agent. Confirm current availability, documentation, pricing, and terms directly before relying on the workflow.

## Watch list

- deployment image
- tool permissions
- channel integration and backup procedure

## Log format

| Checked at | Source | Observed change | Affected assumption | Retest required | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Primary source URL | Concise evidence | Pilot, cost, data, integration, or quality | Yes / No, with reason | Named role |

## Monitoring routine

Check primary documentation and terms on a fixed cadence and before a consequential use. Preserve the source URL, access date, and a short diff or screenshot reference. Treat search snippets and directory descriptions as discovery leads only.

## Retest triggers

Rerun the two onboarding tasks when a watched item changes, a previously accepted output regresses, or a new boundary receives data. Reconfirm permission discipline, audit-log completeness, recovery success, operator review time and keep the old result for comparison.

## Escalation

Pause use when a material change has no owner, a prior acceptance claim can no longer be reproduced, or the updated terms alter rights, privacy, cost, safety, or compliance assumptions.
